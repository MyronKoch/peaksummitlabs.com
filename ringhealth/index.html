Free iPhone app

Your ring. Your phone. Nobody else.

Ringhaven is a free, private companion app for low-cost SmartHealth smart rings. Heart rate, sleep, blood oxygen estimates, breathing, HRV and stress, read straight from the ring and kept on your iPhone. No account, no cloud, no tracking.

Why it exists

These rings cost about as much as lunch, and they measure real things. The app they ship with wants you to create an account first. Ringhaven talks to the ring directly over Bluetooth and keeps everything on your phone, with optional copies to Apple Health and to a backup folder you choose.

Built by one person for himself and his dad. It is free and stays free.

Which rings work

Ringhaven works with rings built on the Yucheng SmartHealth platform. The model name on the listing does not matter (sellers reuse names); what matters is that the box or listing says to use the SmartHealth app. If you pair a ring that is not compatible, Ringhaven tells you right away instead of half-working.

How to check before you buy

  • The listing, the box or the manual names the SmartHealth app. Many listings hide the app name in the photos or the questions section, so look there too.
  • Skip listings that name QRing, Da Rings, Colmi Fit, JRing, KeepFit, RWfit, Fitpro, Wearfit or HBand.
  • If a ring turns out not to be compatible, Ringhaven tells you the first time it connects.

Support

The ring won't connect

Put the ring on its charger for a minute to wake it, keep it near the phone, and make sure the original SmartHealth app is closed (a ring talks to one app at a time). Then open Ringhaven and tap Connect.

Last night is missing

The ring stores readings while you sleep and Ringhaven collects them the next time the app is open and the ring is nearby. If the ring was not worn, or was on the charger, there is nothing to collect.

Moving to a new phone

In Ringhaven, open Data, then Backup, and choose a folder. The app saves a copy once a day. On the new phone, install Ringhaven and tap "Restore from a backup".

Are the numbers medical-grade?

No. A ring's optical sensor gives useful trends, especially for heart rate and sleep, but blood pressure and blood oxygen from a ring are estimates. Ringhaven is a wellness app, not a medical device.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23 (app renamed Ringhaven; storage wording updated)

Ringhaven is a private, on-device companion app for compatible Bluetooth smart rings. It has no accounts, no servers, no advertising, and no analytics. This page explains exactly what the app touches and where it goes.

Short version: your data stays on your iPhone. Ringhaven never sends it to the developer or to anyone else.

What Ringhaven reads from your ring

When you pair a compatible ring over Bluetooth, the app reads the sensor data the ring records:

  • heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, blood pressure estimates, and stress readings
  • step counts and activity
  • sleep sessions and sleep stages
  • battery level and firmware version of the ring
  • the ring's Bluetooth identifier, saved so the app can reconnect to your ring automatically

You can also enter a few details yourself: age, sex, weight, and an optional blood pressure calibration reading from your own cuff. Habit tags and workout sessions you record are stored too.

Where the data is stored

  • All of the above is stored only on your device, in the app's own database. The database files use iOS Data Protection: they are encrypted on the device and cannot be read after a restart until you unlock your iPhone for the first time. After that the app can keep saving your ring's readings overnight while the phone is locked.
  • Small settings (your profile, alert thresholds, night mode times, calibration) are stored in the app's on-device preferences.
  • A small summary (for example your latest health score) is shared with the Ringhaven home screen widget and the Ringhaven Apple Watch app on your own devices. The widget uses an on-device app group. The Watch app is fed by Apple's on-device WatchConnectivity link. Neither involves any server.

Ringhaven makes no network connections. It contains no code that contacts the internet, and it includes no third-party SDKs: no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking. The only web links in the app (this page and the list of compatible rings) open in Safari when you tap them; the app itself sends nothing.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you allow it, Ringhaven connects to Apple Health.

  • Written to Apple Health (with your permission): heart rate, blood pressure, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, heart rate variability, sleep stages, workouts and active energy you record with the app. Steps are not written: your iPhone and Apple Watch already count them.
  • Read from Apple Health (with your permission): body weight, resting heart rate, active energy, workouts, and Apple Watch sleeping wrist temperature, so they can be shown next to your ring's data.

You choose each category in the iOS permission sheet and can change it at any time in Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Ringhaven. The app keeps working with Health access denied.

Health data is used only to show you your own information inside the app. It is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and it is never shared with third parties. Ringhaven does not use iCloud itself. Your data only leaves the app as a file you export or a backup folder you choose (see Backups).

Notifications

If you enable alerts, Ringhaven schedules local notifications on your phone (for example when two readings in a row fall outside limits you set). They are generated on the device. Nothing is sent to a notification server.

Export and sharing

Ringhaven can create CSV, JSON, and PDF files of your data. This happens only when you tap an export or share button, and the file goes only where you send it using the standard iOS share sheet (Files, AirDrop, Mail, and so on). Once you share a file, the receiving app or person is governed by their own policies.

Backups

You can choose a folder in the Files app and Ringhaven will save a copy of your history there once a day, as a single file, so you can restore it on a new phone. Where that folder lives (on the phone, on a drive, or in a cloud service you use) is your choice, and you can stop at any time. Ringhaven never sends the file anywhere else.

If you use iCloud Backup or an encrypted Finder/iTunes backup for your whole device, the app's data may be included in that device backup, like any other app's data. That backup is controlled by your Apple settings, not by Ringhaven.

Third parties

None. Ringhaven has no developer-operated servers and shares nothing with third parties. Apple's own frameworks (Bluetooth, HealthKit, notifications) operate on your device under your permissions.

Children

Ringhaven is not directed to children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.

Your controls and deleting your data

  • Stop collecting: tap Disconnect Ring in the app Settings, or turn off Bluetooth for the app in iOS Settings.
  • Revoke Health access: Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Ringhaven.
  • Delete data stored by the app: delete the Ringhaven app from your iPhone. This removes the app's database, preferences, and widget data. (Uninstalling also removes the data from the Watch app.)
  • Delete data the app wrote to Apple Health: open the Health app, choose the data type (for example Heart Rate), then Show All Data, and delete entries. You can also delete by source under Profile > Apps > Ringhaven > Delete All Data from "Ringhaven".
  • Delete exports you made: delete the files from wherever you saved or sent them.
  • Delete data on the ring: the ring's stored history is cleared after the app has safely saved it to your phone.

Not medical advice

Ringhaven is for general wellness and informational use. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, and its readings and estimates (including blood pressure, blood oxygen, sleep, and scores) can be wrong. Do not use it to make medical decisions. Talk to a qualified healthcare professional about health concerns, and call your local emergency number in an emergency.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new date. Because the app collects nothing off your device, a material change would require an app update that you would have to install.
