Jan's Recipe Box

Every recipe in one box. Your box.

Free for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Jan's Recipe Box keeps your recipes in one place, on your iPhone, iPad and Mac, with a Chrome extension that clips a recipe from any website in one click. Everything is saved in your own Google Drive. We never see it.

What it does

  • Clip from any recipe site. One click in Chrome saves just the recipe: no ads, no life story.
  • Cook Mode. Big text, one step at a time, and the screen stays on while you cook.
  • What can I make? Search by the ingredients you have, several at once.
  • Categories and servings. Sort recipes your way and scale any recipe up or down.
  • Share a recipe card with a friend.
  • YouTube cooking videos. Click the Chrome button on a cooking video and the app makes the recipe: from the creator's written recipe, the video's description, or, on devices with Apple Intelligence, from what's said in the video, written on your device. A "Watch the Video" button stays on the recipe.
  • From a Photo. Take a picture of a recipe in a newspaper or a printout. The app reads it on your device, and you check the amounts before saving. The photo becomes the recipe's picture.
  • My Websites. Keep your favorite recipe sites one click away.
  • Sample recipes in every category, so a new box isn't empty. Delete them anytime.

Built by Myron for his mom Jan's 82nd birthday. It's free, with no ads.

Get the Chrome button

The Chrome button saves the recipe from any web page into your Recipe Box. Here's how to add it to Chrome on your computer. It takes about two minutes.

Download the Chrome button

  1. Click the download button above. The file goes to your Downloads folder.
  2. Open Downloads and double-click Jans-Recipe-Box-Chrome.zip. A folder called Jans-Recipe-Box-Chrome appears. Drag that folder into your Documents folder and leave it there. (If the folder is deleted, the button stops working.)
  3. In Chrome, click the address bar at the top, type chrome://extensions and press Return.
  4. Turn on Developer mode with the switch at the top right. Leave it on.
  5. Click Load unpacked at the top left, choose the Jans-Recipe-Box-Chrome folder in Documents, and click Select.
  6. Click the puzzle-piece icon to the right of the address bar, then click the pin next to Jan's Recipe Box, so the button always shows.
  7. Open any recipe page and click the Recipe Box button.

Also in the Chrome Web Store: add Jan's Recipe Box to Chrome in one click. The download above gets new features first.

Support

Getting it

Jan's Recipe Box is with Apple for review now. When it's approved, search the App Store for Jan's Recipe Box (iPhone, iPad and Mac). The Chrome extension adds a button to Chrome that saves the recipe on the page you're looking at.

Where are my recipes?

In a folder called Recipe Box in your own Google Drive. The app also keeps a copy on your device so your recipes and photos work offline.

A recipe didn't clip properly

Some sites lay recipes out in unusual ways. Email us the page address and we'll take a look.

Saving a YouTube recipe

On the YouTube video's page, click the Recipe Box button, then open the recipe in the app. If the app asks you to check the amounts, look them over before you save.

Adding a recipe from a photo

Choose Add a Recipe, then From a Photo. Take a picture or pick one, then check the amounts before you save.

I don't see my recipes on my iPad

Sign in with the same Google account you use on your other devices, then tap Update My Recipes.

Moving to a new device

Install the app and sign in with the same Google account. Your recipes come back from your Drive.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22 (photos, Apple Intelligence, sample recipes, My Websites and the extension's Google Doc copy added)

This policy covers the Jan's Recipe Box app for iPhone, iPad and Mac and the Jan's Recipe Box Chrome extension, both made by Peak Summit Labs.

Short version: your recipes are stored in your own Google Drive and on your own devices. Peak Summit Labs runs no server for this app and never receives, stores or sees your recipes or your personal data.

Signing in with Google

You sign in with your Google account through Google's own sign-in. We never see your password.

What Google data the app accesses

  • Google Drive, limited to files the app creates (the drive.file scope). The app can see and change only the recipe files and photos it saves to your Recipe Box folder. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Your basic Google profile (name and email address), which the app's Google sign-in provides. It stays on your device and is used only to show which account is signed in and to keep two accounts' recipes apart. The Chrome extension asks only for the Drive permission.

How that data is used

The app uses this access only to save your recipes, photos, recipe Docs and My Websites list to your Drive, and to load them back so you can view, search and cook from them on your devices. Nothing else.

Sharing and transfer

Your Google data is not shared with, transferred to, or sold to anyone. It is not used for advertising, and no person at Peak Summit Labs reads it. A recipe card leaves the app only when you choose to share it, through your device's share sheet, to the person or app you pick.

Jan's Recipe Box's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The Chrome extension

The extension reads the web page you are on only when you click its button, to pick out the recipe. It sends that recipe to your own Google Drive and nowhere else. It does not watch your browsing. On a YouTube cooking video, it also reads that video's page and captions from YouTube itself, to find the recipe. It keeps a short list of the page addresses you've saved, in Chrome's own storage on your computer, so it can tell you when you've saved a recipe before.

By default, the extension also saves a Google Doc copy of each recipe in your Recipe Box folder, so you can print or share it outside the app. You can turn this off in the extension's Settings. "Open in Jan's Recipes" passes the saved recipe's ID to the app on the same computer, and nothing else.

Photos and the camera

"From a Photo" reads the recipe in your picture on your device, using Apple's on-device text recognition. The first photo is saved to your own Google Drive (Recipe Box/Photos) as the recipe's picture. The app uses the camera only when you tap "Take a Photo Now". When you choose an existing photo, Apple's photo picker shares only the photo you pick.

Apple Intelligence

On devices that have Apple Intelligence, recipe text from a photo or from a YouTube video's captions is processed by Apple's on-device model. It stays on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to any third-party AI service.

Sample recipes and My Websites

The first time you sign in with an empty box, the app adds sample recipes and their photos to your own Drive (Recipe Box/Photos). You can delete them anytime. The sample photos come from Wikimedia Commons under their licenses, and are credited in the app's About screen. Your My Websites list is saved as a file in your Recipe Box folder in your own Drive.

Recipe websites

When you add a recipe by its web address, the app fetches that page to read the recipe, and it downloads the recipe's photos from the original website. Those websites see an ordinary request from your device, just as they would if you visited them in a browser. Nothing else is sent to them.

Data on your device

The app keeps a local copy of your recipes and photos on your device so they work without a connection. It writes diagnostic messages to your device's own system log; these never include your sign-in tokens and are never uploaded. There are no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Data on a device: delete the app from that device. To remove the extension, remove it from Chrome.
  • A single recipe: delete it in the app. Its file moves to your Google Drive Trash.
  • Data in your Drive: delete the Recipe Box folder from your Google Drive.
  • Google access: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
