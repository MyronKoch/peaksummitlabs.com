Free for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Every recipe in one box. Your box.

Jan's Recipe Box keeps your recipes in one place, on your iPhone, iPad and Mac, with a Chrome extension that clips a recipe from any website in one click. Everything is saved in your own Google Drive. We never see it.

What it does

  • Clip from any recipe site. One click in Chrome saves just the recipe: no ads, no life story.
  • Cook Mode. Big text, one step at a time, and the screen stays on while you cook.
  • What can I make? Search by the ingredients you have, several at once.
  • Categories and servings. Sort recipes your way and scale any recipe up or down.
  • Share a recipe card with a friend.

Built by Myron for his mom Jan's birthday. It's free, with no ads.

Support

Getting it

The app is in testing on Apple's TestFlight for iPhone, iPad and Mac, with an App Store release to follow. The Chrome extension adds a button to Chrome that saves the recipe on the page you're looking at.

Where are my recipes?

In a folder called Recipe Box in your own Google Drive. The app also keeps a copy on your device so your recipes and photos work offline.

A recipe didn't clip properly

Some sites lay recipes out in unusual ways. Email us the page address and we'll take a look.

Moving to a new device

Install the app and sign in with the same Google account. Your recipes come back from your Drive.

Contact

Email support@peaksummitlabs.com. A real person reads it.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22

This policy covers the Jan's Recipe Box app for iPhone, iPad and Mac and the Jan's Recipe Box Chrome extension, both made by Peak Summit Labs.

Short version: your recipes are stored in your own Google Drive and on your own devices. Peak Summit Labs runs no server for this app and never receives, stores or sees your recipes or your personal data.

Signing in with Google

You sign in with your Google account through Google's own sign-in. We never see your password.

What Google data the app accesses

The app asks for one Google permission: Google Drive, limited to files the app creates (the drive.file scope). It can see and change only the recipe files and photos it saves to your Recipe Box folder. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

How that data is used

The app uses this access only to save your recipes and photos to your Drive, and to load them back so you can view, search and cook from them on your devices. Nothing else.

Sharing and transfer

Your Google data is not shared with, transferred to, or sold to anyone. It is not used for advertising, and no person at Peak Summit Labs reads it. A recipe card leaves the app only when you choose to share it, through your device's share sheet, to the person or app you pick.

Jan's Recipe Box's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The Chrome extension

The extension reads the web page you are on only when you click its button, to pick out the recipe. It sends that recipe to your own Google Drive and nowhere else. It does not watch your browsing.

Data on your device

The app keeps a local copy of your recipes and photos on your device so they work without a connection. There are no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Data on a device: delete the app from that device. To remove the extension, remove it from Chrome.
  • Data in your Drive: delete the Recipe Box folder from your Google Drive.
  • Google access: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
